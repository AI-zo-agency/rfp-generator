COMPLIANCE
--------------------------------------------------

Create one compliance item for each independently answerable bidder obligation.

Look for requirements to:
submit, provide, include, identify, describe, explain, list,
confirm, acknowledge, complete, sign, attach, certify,
demonstrate, disclose, upload, organize, format or label.

Do not collapse separately answerable sub-requirements.

Preserve conditionality.

If a requirement applies only if:
- subcontractors are used
- proprietary information is submitted
- exceptions are taken
- the bidder is selected for award
- a specific option/condition applies

set:
"mandatory": false

and state the condition explicitly in the requirement.

Separate bidder-response requirements from post-award obligations using targetSection and owner.

POST-AWARD in compliance.items: mandatory MUST be false (W-9 after selection, insurance after award,
monthly status reports during performance). These may ALSO appear in scope.mandatory as performance
obligations — do not confuse bidder-response mandatory with contract-performance mandatory.

Owners: bidder | selectedBidder | contractor | county | subcontractor.
Post-award targetSection must start with "Post-Award / ...".

Use obligationCandidates and langextract.complianceHits as a recall aid — classify/normalize them.
Every LangExtract compliance candidate MUST appear in compliance.items OR be explicitly reclassified
with a recorded reason (never silent drop). Target full submittal/required-forms coverage, not a short summary list.

--------------------------------------------------
SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------

Exhaustively classify SOW/substantive attachments into scope (use sowExcerpt + scopeCandidates).

mandatory:
required contract performance (shall/must/will/is responsible/required to).

optional:
genuinely as-needed, at-request, may/could optional County services — NOT bidder delivery choices
(e.g. subcontractor use is NOT scope.optional).

dependencies:
County approvals, COR, task orders, systems, inputs, coordination, permits, existing platforms.

futurePhases:
explicit option years or later phases.

outOfScope:
only explicit exclusions/boundaries.

dependencies:
approvals, systems, County inputs, regulatory dependencies,
task authorizations and coordination requirements.

If different sections conflict, preserve the conflict in scope.notes
or the nearest notes field.
