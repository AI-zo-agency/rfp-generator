--------------------------------------------------
COMPLIANCE
--------------------------------------------------

Create one compliance item for each independently answerable bidder obligation.

Look for requirements to:
submit, provide, include, identify, describe, explain, list,
confirm, acknowledge, complete, sign, attach, certify,
demonstrate, disclose, upload, organize, format or label.

Do not collapse separately answerable sub-requirements.

Preserve conditionality.

If a requirement applies only if:
- subcontractors are used
- proprietary information is submitted
- exceptions are taken
- the bidder is selected for award
- a specific option/condition applies

set:
"mandatory": false

and state the condition explicitly in the requirement.

Separate bidder-response requirements from post-award obligations using targetSection and owner.

Use obligationCandidates from the evidence pack as a recall aid — classify/normalize them;
do not drop real candidates without reason; you may add obligations the pack missed if evidence supports them.

only explicit exclusions/boundaries.

or the nearest notes field.

--------------------------------------------------
HIGH-RISK FIDELITY
--------------------------------------------------

Verify exact meaning of:
- numbers
- dates
- years
- quantities
- frequencies
