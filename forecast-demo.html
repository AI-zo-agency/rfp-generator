How this is calculated

no model training, no new service
  1. 1
    Collection curve from qb_txn_links — 762 paid invoices, $3.37M. Half collect by day 17, 96% by day 60.
  2. 2
    Run off open AR — each of the 17 open invoices ages along that curve, conditioned on how old it already is.
  3. 3
    Add forecast billing from the revenue model, collected on the same curve. Flagged separately so the two never blur.
  4. 4
    Subtract outflow — $133k/mo, calibrated from collections minus actual cash movement, plus dated bills.
  5. 5
    Band from back-test residuals, not from a confidence interval the model invented about itself.

Runs inside the existing 11pm sync as one more panel in build_overview. Adds roughly 400 lines of pure Python. No Prophet, no scikit-learn — 32 monthly data points cannot support a learned model, and pretending otherwise is how forecasts get trusted right up until they are wrong.
